Constitution Gardens

Fifty tranquil acres of shade trees, winding paths and a quiet lake, tucked between the Vietnam Veterans Memorial and the World War II Memorial.

Plan your visit

A quiet green room on the Mall

Constitution Gardens is a landscaped park on the north side of the Reflecting Pool, a calm contrast to the monuments around it. It is part of National Mall and Memorial Parks, managed by the National Park Service.

Things to see & do

Wander, picnic, read or simply sit by the water.

🏛️

Signers Memorial

On a small island in the lake, the Memorial to the 56 Signers of the Declaration of Independence displays each signer’s name and signature in granite.

🦆

The Lake

A 6.75-acre lake with a curving shoreline and a half-acre island. Ducks and geese gather here, and the Washington Monument reflects in the water.

🌳

Shaded Paths

Winding walks pass under mature trees, with benches and lawns for resting between memorial visits.

🧺

Picnics & Pauses

Open lawns and lakeside seating make an easy lunch stop during a day of sightseeing.

🚶

Walking Route

Connects naturally to the Vietnam Veterans Memorial, Lincoln Memorial and the Washington Monument grounds.

📷

Photography

Early morning and golden hour bring soft light, reflections and fewer crowds.

A short history

Built as part of the nation’s Bicentennial celebrations.

  • Late 19th century — The park’s 50 acres were originally beneath the Potomac River, before the riverfront was reclaimed as land.
  • 1930s–1940s — A flood-control levee was built along the park’s south edge, shaping the site visitors see today.
  • May 1976 — Constitution Gardens was dedicated during the Bicentennial. It has been a separate park unit since 1982.
  • 1984 — The Memorial to the 56 Signers of the Declaration of Independence was dedicated on the lake island.
  • 2023 — The Trust for the National Mall awarded Phase II of the gardens’ rehabilitation, improving soil, drainage and plantings.

Getting there
Walk from the Smithsonian or Foggy Bottom–GWU Metro stations (Blue/Orange/Silver lines), or take the DC Circulator.
Parking
Very limited near the Mall; public transit or walking is easiest.
Good to know
Bring water and sun protection in summer. Paths are mostly paved and level.
